# Phase 24.12 — Snapshot, Filesystem & State Preservation Integration

## Objective

Integrate platform evolution with actual storage/filesystem preservation capabilities.

Discover filesystem types, mount topology, LUKS/LVM/RAID where visible, snapshot-capable layers, boot partition constraints, /home separation, and backup/snapshot availability.

Model what a snapshot does and does not protect. Avoid assuming Btrfs/ZFS/LVM snapshots exist. Do not create/delete snapshots unless separately authorized. Feed verified preservation capabilities into recovery contracts.
