# Intent, Context, Capability and Authority

Keep four concepts distinct:
1. Intent — what appears to be requested.
2. Context — what the request plausibly refers to here and now.
3. Capability — what Rebuntu can represent and perform through typed interfaces.
4. Authority — what this requester/session/policy may authorize.

Natural language may broaden understanding; it must never broaden authority.

An executable candidate requires sufficient intent resolution, contextual legitimacy, available typed capability, applicable policy, authorization and safety validation.
