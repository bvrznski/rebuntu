# Gordon Advisory Consultation

Gordon may be consulted for ambiguous, unusual or contextually inconsistent requests when configured and available.

Provide Gordon only a bounded, redacted EvidenceBundle containing the minimum relevant state, request, policy facts and justification. Gordon may identify inconsistencies, risks, missing evidence and useful clarification questions.

`Gordon verdict != authorization`.
Gordon cannot grant capabilities, weaken policy, execute commands, fabricate observations or turn UNKNOWN into PASS. Rebuntu remains fully functional and safe when Gordon is unavailable.
