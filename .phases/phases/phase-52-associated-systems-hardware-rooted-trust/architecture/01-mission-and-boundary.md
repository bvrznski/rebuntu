# Phase 52 — Associated Systems & Hardware-Rooted Trust

Phase 52 lets autonomous Rebuntu systems establish explicit cryptographic associations without collapsing them into one administrative fabric.

Phase 51: nodes inside one administrative Rebuntu fabric.
Phase 52: autonomous systems/realms establishing bounded bilateral trust.
Future Phase 53: federation among associated systems/realms.

Core rule:
`authentication != association != authorization`

An association identifies a verified peer relationship and its provenance. It never grants unrestricted authority by itself.
