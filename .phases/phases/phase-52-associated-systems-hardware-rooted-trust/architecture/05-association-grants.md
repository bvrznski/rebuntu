# Association Grants

A system may issue a bounded AssociationGrant permitting another system to initiate an association.

A grant is cryptographically protected, scoped, expiring where appropriate, revocable where feasible and optionally single-use.

Possible transports include QR, file, removable media, NFC or other channels.

Possession of a grant permits only the operation encoded by the grant; it is not general system authority.
