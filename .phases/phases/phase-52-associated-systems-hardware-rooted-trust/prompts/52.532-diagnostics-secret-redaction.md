## Fundamental model
Phase 52 associates autonomous Rebuntu systems or realms. It does **not** merge them into one Phase 51 administrative fabric.

Preserve these distinctions:

`authentication != association != authorization`
`connectivity != trust`
`association != unrestricted trust`
`possession of an invitation != general authority`
`transport != trust`
`hardware authenticator != session key`
`graph relation != authorization`
`semantic recommendation != authorization`
`UNKNOWN != PASS`

A peer remains a separate trust domain.

## Cryptographic contract
Use modern, reviewed cryptographic protocols/libraries. Do not invent cryptographic primitives or ad-hoc constructions.

Key agreement and authentication are separate. A Diffie-Hellman-style exchange alone does not prove peer identity. Where appropriate, use authenticated ephemeral key agreement and transcript binding to obtain peer authentication plus forward secrecy.

Bind identities, protocol version/algorithms, nonces, requested scope and relevant context into the authenticated handshake/transcript. Resist MITM, replay, downgrade, unknown-key-share, identity substitution and cross-protocol confusion.

Private identity keys must never be copied to peers as an association mechanism.

## Ceremony and hardware
Association is established through a typed `AssociationCeremony`.

Ceremony providers may use QR, short authentication strings, files/removable media, NFC/proximity, authenticated network exchange, existing identity systems, FIDO2/WebAuthn/U2F where semantically valid, smartcards/PIV, TPM-backed identities or other future providers.

Hardware is optional assurance, not the architecture.

Never assume FIDO/U2F private credentials are exportable, clonable or arbitrary DH keys. Hardware presence may authorize issuance/acceptance/change/revocation while the systems establish their own authenticated session keys.

## Association grants
Support bounded cryptographically protected `AssociationGrant` semantics where relevant: issuer, nonce, purpose, proposed TrustScope, recipient constraints, expiry, use count, provenance and revocation/consumption state.

A grant may travel through QR/file/removable media/etc. Its transport does not itself establish trust.

Grant possession permits only the explicitly encoded association operation and cannot silently broaden permissions.

## TrustScope and authority
Association creates an authenticated peer relationship. `TrustScope` expresses the maximum intended relationship scope, not automatic execution authority.

Phase 47 policy and Phase 45 authorization remain authoritative for consequential actions. Each autonomous system evaluates its own policy. Cross-system policy combination must never silently broaden either side.

Consequential remote execution continues to require typed plans, fresh target-side validation, authorization, execution, verification and Phase 39 recording.
